Redesign Implementation: Student Portal Mascot Reminder (sidebar)

Keep the current Go Learning brand: dark navy sidebar, indigo accent, Plus Jakarta Sans, rounded cards, light and dark mode. Attach these assets with this prompt: the 10 mascot images (reminder_01 to reminder_10.png) and the speech bubble image (thots_clouds.jpg).

1. PLACEMENT
- Put the mascot in the sidebar, in the empty space above the streak card (bottom of the sidebar).
- The mascot stands under a speech bubble. The bubble tail points down-left toward the mascot's head.
- Tapping the mascot hides or shows the bubble.
- On phones the sidebar becomes a top row, so show the mascot smaller (about 60px) with the bubble under the logo and menu.

2. SPEECH BUBBLE (use the uploaded bubble image)
- Remove the white background from the image so it is transparent. Use it as the background of the message box, stretched to fit.
- Make 3 color versions of the bubble: white (welcome and tips), light red (overdue), light yellow (exam).
- The message text must always fit inside the white area, above the tail:
  - Start at 13px and shrink in steps to a minimum of about 10.5px.
  - If it still does not fit, make the bubble taller instead of letting text overflow.
  - Re-fit when the window resizes and after the font loads.
- The tag chips and the "Start now" button scale with the text size.

3. TEN POSES AND THEIR MESSAGES (12 messages, so clipboard and thinking carry two each)
- Calendar: "Hey there! Don't forget to submit your assignments before the deadline! Let's get that A+!"
- Alarm clock: "Psst! You have pending homework due soon. Let's finish it early so you can relax later!"
- Megaphone: "Oras na para mag-aral! Huwag kalimutang tapusin ang iyong takdang-aralin bago ang takdang araw."
- "REMINDER!" sign: "Double-check your tasks tab! A finished homework is a stress-free day."
- Bell: "Keep your streak alive! Complete at least one activity today to earn bonus points!"
- Checkmark: "Great job logging in today! You're one step closer to topping the leaderboard! 🏆"
- Cheering: "Galing mo! Ipagpatuloy ang iyong daily streak para sa mas maraming badges at XP!"
- Lightbulb: "Every question you practice makes you smarter and levels up your brain!"
- Clipboard: "Take your time! Read each question and choices carefully before clicking your answer." and "For Identification questions, check your spelling carefully before hitting Submit ↵!"
- Thinking: "Need help understanding the question? Try clicking the language translator button (🌐) at the top!" and "Huwag magmadali! Basahing mabuti ang bawat tanong bago sumagot."
- The first four messages are about pending homework. Show them only when the student really has pending work.

4. RANDOM ROTATION
- Switch to a new pose and message about every 9 seconds, picked at random.
- Never show the same message twice in a row.
- Pause while the student hovers over the mascot, while a homework or exam is being answered, when the bubble is hidden, and when the browser tab is in the background.
- Fade the bubble and mascot in on each change. Turn the animation off when reduced motion is on.

5. WELCOME BY NAME
- The first message when the page opens is a welcome with the cheering pose: "Good afternoon, Ed Ryan! 👋 Welcome back to Go Learning. You have 3 tasks to finish." If nothing is pending: "You are all caught up!"
- The greeting changes by the student's local time: Good morning, Good afternoon, Good evening.
- Use the logged-in student's first name and full name from their profile, not hard-coded text.
- About 4 in 10 random tips start with "Hi {first name}!".
- The Classes page header reads "STUDENT WORKSPACE", then "Good afternoon, {first name}! 👋", then "Welcome back, {full name}." with a "My classes" heading above the class cards.
- The homework results screen says "Nice work, {first name}! Here is your score."

6. HOMEWORK AND EXAM NOTIFICATIONS (highest priority)
- When the student has pending work, the bubble alternates: a notification, then a random tip, then the next notification, and so on.
- Notifications, each with a tag chip and a "Start now" button that opens that homework or exam in the player:
  - Overdue (red bubble, alarm clock): "{title} was due {date}. Please finish it now!"
  - Exam alert (yellow bubble, "REMINDER!" sign): "{title} is on {date and time}. Review your lessons first!"
  - Homework (white bubble, calendar): "You have {n} homework to do. Next: {title}, due {date}."
- Show a red count badge on the "Classes" menu item with the number of pending homework and exams.
- Exams are a type of homework: the list shows an EXAM label instead of HW, the Homework tab is named "Homework & Exams", and the player says "Start exam" and "Submit exam".
- When the student submits something, update the notifications and the badge right away.

7. ACCESSIBILITY AND QUALITY
- The mascot button has an accessible label such as "Show or hide reminder".
- Do not let the rotating text steal keyboard focus.
- Keep the page fast: compress the mascot images and the bubble images.
- Do not change unrelated pages.
